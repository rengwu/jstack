Retained: a decision tree, rounds of ready questions, recommended answers, research of discoverable facts, and follow-ups driven by the user's answers.

jstack adaptation: at most ten questions per round (usually six to ten when useful), no session-wide limit, sufficient-clarity stopping with explicit deferrals, and persistence through plan and the target project's wiki. The skill is self-contained and does not depend on an upstream Skill tool, the separate `grilling` skill, or mandatory subagents. Upstream changes should be reviewed against these choices before incorporation; do not overwrite this adaptation automatically.
